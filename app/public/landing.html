The problem问题在哪

Every pre-service teacher must do micro-teaching. Almost none of them get told where it will break. 每个师范生都要试讲，但几乎没人告诉他们：哪里会翻车。

试讲的反馈通常是三句话：「讲得不错」「再自信一点」「多互动」。听起来都对，但下一次上台，你还是不知道哪一句让学生走神、哪个问题抛出去会冷场、哪一段语速快到学生跟不上。

更麻烦的是压力。真实课堂的压迫感无法预演——学生追问、后排起哄、投影突然黑掉，这些场景在实习前一节课都没练过，第一次遇到就是真课堂。

于是我们把这件事拆成两半：一半用数据量化（语速、等待、口头禅、互动结构），一半用 AI 演出来（三个性格迥异的学生当场拆台）。

Feedback on micro-teaching is usually three sentences: "good job", "be more confident", "interact more". All true, and all useless — because next time you still don't know which sentence lost the class, which question landed in silence, or which segment ran too fast to follow.

Worse is the pressure. Real classroom pressure cannot be rehearsed — a student pushing back, the back row acting up, the projector dying mid-derivation. None of it is practised before the internship, and the first time is the real thing.

So we split the problem in two: quantify one half with data (pace, wait time, filler words, interaction structure) and perform the other half with AI (three students with distinct personalities who push back in real time).

How it works三步就能跑完

Paste a script. Read the report. Face the students. 粘一段讲稿 → 看一份报告 → 面对三个学生。

1

Paste your script粘讲稿

Type it, dictate it, or upload a video and let it transcribe. A lesson plan can be attached for coverage checking.打字、语音输入，或上传讲课视频自动转写；还能贴一份教案，检查知识点覆盖。

"So today we'll learn fraction division…"「同学们好，今天我们学习分数除法……」
2

Read the report看报告

Score, danger moments, filler ranking, wait-time heatmap, time allocation, curriculum alignment, iFIAS matrix, and a rewritable script.分数、危险瞬间、口头禅排行、等待热力图、时间分配、课标对照、iFIAS 矩阵，还有一份可直接照着练的改进稿。

"Pause 0.5s → students had no time to think."「提问后只等了 0.5 秒，学生来不及想。」
3

Face the students面对学生

Three AI students — the nitpicker, the over-achiever, the daydreamer — ask questions, quote your own words back, and drive a pressure meter up.小皮抬杠、小霸追问原理、小走走神。他们会引用你刚说过的话反问，压力条一路往上顶。

"You just said 『multiply by the reciprocal』— then what about dividing by zero?"「老师你刚才说『除以一个数等于乘倒数』，那除以 0 呢？」

Under the hood技术实现

Zero-dependency Node backend + static front end; every AI feature has a rule-based fallback, so a dead network never kills a demo. 零依赖 Node 后端 + 静态前端；每个 AI 能力都有规则兜底，断网也演得下去。

Step 01

Segment切分话语单元

Split by sentence-final punctuation, then by commas for over-long turns.按句末标点切分，过长句再按逗号切，上限 80 句。

Step 02

Code逐句编码

LLM coder at temperature 0.2; keyword and punctuation rules as fallback.大模型低温编码；关键词与标点规则兜底。

Step 03

Matrix互动矩阵

Adjacent-transition counts, steady-state diagonal, content-cross ratio.相邻转移计数、对角稳态格、内容十字区比率。

Step 04

Cluster + project聚类与降维

7-D sliding-window features → DBSCAN with adaptive ε → Jacobi PCA to 2-D.7 维滑窗特征 → 自适应 ε 的 DBSCAN → Jacobi PCA 降到 2 维。

Ethics & limits伦理与边界

This is a training tool for pre-service teachers, not a judge of who is fit to teach. 这是给师范生练习用的工具，不是判断谁「配不配当老师」的裁判。

  • 本地保存 ≠ 不联网。讲稿、报告、历史记录默认存在浏览器本地；但只要使用在线分析，内容就会发送到应用后端，启用外部模型或云端转写时还会发送至对应服务提供方。登录后，报告与讲稿正文会存档到云端。
  • 树洞是公开共享的。你发布的帖子正文、AI 回复与学校名会保存到云端并对所有用户展示（不展示昵称与账号，但仍可举报下架）。服务端可能通过账号关联识别发布者，因此它是「不显示身份」而非「无法追溯」。不登录则仅保存在本设备。
  • 分数不是评价，是参照。我们不承诺绝对分数，只承诺相对变化——同一个人的两次试讲放在一起看趋势，才是能力成长的证据。每项分数旁边都给可验证的依据：原句、秒数、次数。
  • 模拟反馈不是现实测量。AI 学生的专注、理解与压力值是模拟课堂状态，不是对真实学生心理的测量；六视角评价是模拟角色，不是真实督导、家长或同行意见。
  • 不用于替代真实教学评价。AI 反馈不能代替导师评课、学生反馈与真实课堂经验。它的位置是「上台之前的第二次机会」。
  • 告警与纠错。如发现内容被误判、被滥用，或涉及隐私问题，请联系项目团队，我们会修正或下架相关内容。
  • Local storage is not offline. Scripts, reports and history live in the browser by default, but any online analysis sends content to our server, and external model or cloud transcription providers may receive it too. Once signed in, reports and script text are archived in the cloud.
  • The hollow is shared publicly. Your post, the AI reply and your school name are stored in the cloud and shown to all users (no nickname or account is displayed, but you can report a post for removal, and the server may still link a post to your account). Without signing in, posts stay on your device only.
  • Scores are a reference, not a verdict. We promise relative change, not absolute truth: two attempts by the same person, read together, are the evidence. Every score ships with the sentence, seconds or counts behind it.
  • Simulated feedback is not a real measurement. AI-student attention, comprehension and stress values simulate classroom states; they are not measurements of real students. The six-perspective review uses simulated roles, not real supervisors, parents or peers.
  • Not a substitute for real evaluation. AI feedback cannot replace mentor reviews, peer feedback, or classroom experience. Its place is the second chance before the real class.
  • Corrections welcome. If something is misjudged, misused, or privacy-sensitive, contact the team and we will fix or remove it.
